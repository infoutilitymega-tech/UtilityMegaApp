About EMI Calculator

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed payment you make to the lender each month until the loan is fully repaid. It includes both principal and interest components.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)
P = Principal | r = Monthly rate | n = Months

Tips

A longer tenure reduces EMI but increases total interest. Make part-prepayments when possible to reduce interest burden significantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI? ▼

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment — a fixed amount paid monthly to repay a loan that includes both principal and interest.

Does EMI change? ▼

For fixed-rate loans, EMI stays the same. For floating-rate loans, EMI may change when the interest rate changes.

Can I prepay my loan? ▼

Yes! Prepayment reduces your outstanding principal, which reduces future interest. Most banks allow prepayment with minimal or no penalty.

Home loan vs personal loan EMI? ▼

Home loan rates are typically lower (8–10%) vs personal loans (12–18%), resulting in lower EMI for the same amount and tenure.
