About Loan Calculator

A loan calculator helps you plan repayments for any type of loan — personal, home, car or education. Knowing your EMI helps you budget accordingly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of loans can I calculate? ▼

This calculator works for personal loans, car loans, home loans, education loans — any fixed-rate EMI loan.

How to reduce total interest? ▼

Increase tenure reduces EMI but raises total interest. Making part-prepayments significantly reduces interest cost.
