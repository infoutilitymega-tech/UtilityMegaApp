About Percentage Calculator

Percentage calculations are used everywhere — discounts, taxes, profit margins, exam scores, and more. Our 3-mode calculator covers all common percentage needs.

Formulas

X% of Y = (X/100) × Y
% Change = ((New−Old)/Old) × 100

Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate 15% of 200? ▼

15% of 200 = (15/100) × 200 = 30. Use Mode 1 in our calculator.

How to find percentage increase? ▼

Use Mode 3 (% Change). Enter old value and new value. E.g. from 100 to 120 = 20% increase.
