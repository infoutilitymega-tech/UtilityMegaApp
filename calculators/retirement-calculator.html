About Retirement Calculator

Planning for retirement early is the single most important financial decision you can make. Even small monthly investments, when started early, grow into substantial corpus through compounding.

The Power of Early Start

Starting at 25 vs 35 with ₹10,000/month at 12% return: ₹3.5 Cr vs ₹1 Cr at age 60. A 10-year head start triples the corpus!

Frequently Asked Questions

How much corpus do I need to retire? ▼

A common rule: 25× your annual expenses (4% withdrawal rule). If annual expenses are ₹5L, you need ₹1.25 Cr minimum.

What return rate should I assume? ▼

12% for equity mutual funds (historical average). Use 8% for a conservative estimate. Always account for inflation (6%).
