Complete Guide to SIP Calculators

A SIP Calculator estimates how your systematic investments grow using compounding. UtilityMega offers 5 SIP calculator types — from beginner Regular SIP to advanced XIRR for tracking real portfolio returns.

Regular SIP Formula

M = P × {[(1+r)ⁿ − 1] ÷ r} × (1+r)
M = Maturity  |  P = Monthly SIP  |  r = Monthly rate (Annual÷12)  |  n = Months

Step-Up SIP Advantage

Step-Up SIP increases your monthly investment by a fixed % every year (typically 10%, aligned with salary hikes). Over 20 years, this can produce 2–3× more wealth than a flat SIP with the same starting amount.

What is XIRR?

XIRR (Extended Internal Rate of Return) calculates annualized return for irregular cashflows — the most accurate measure of real portfolio performance used by financial advisors and AMCs.

FAQ — SIP

What is Step-Up SIP? ▼

Step-Up SIP increases your monthly SIP by a fixed % each year. A 10% step-up on ₹5,000 becomes ₹5,500 in year 2, ₹6,050 in year 3 — significantly boosting returns.

SIP vs Lumpsum — which is better? ▼

For most investors, SIP is better due to rupee cost averaging. Lumpsum outperforms only if timed at market lows. Combining both works best.

How accurate is XIRR? ▼

XIRR is the most accurate return measure. Unlike simple returns, it accounts for exact timing and amount of each cashflow, giving true annualized return.

What return rate should I use? ▼

Use 10–12% for diversified equity funds, 6–8% for debt funds, 12–15% for small/mid cap. These are historical averages — actual returns vary.

Is the 15×15×15 rule real? ▼

Yes! ₹15,000/month for 15 years at 15% return ≈ ₹1 crore — demonstrating compounding power for long-term SIP investors.

Can I start SIP with ₹500? ▼

Yes! Many AMCs allow SIP from ₹100–500/month. Consistency matters more than amount — small sums compound significantly over 15–20 years.
